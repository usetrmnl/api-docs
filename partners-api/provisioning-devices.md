# Provisioning Devices

We offer two tools to customize devices before your customers unbox them: Tailor for your brand, and the Preload app for your plugins.

**Step 1 - Add your logo with Tailor**

[Tailor](https://tailor.trmnl.com) puts your own images on the screens a device shows while it starts up. Upload a splash image and a loader image, each up to 800x480 pixels, then flash them to the device over USB-C. This works on OG, BWRY, and X device models.

**Step 2 - Get your plugin ready**

The Preload app installs published plugins and published or unlisted recipes. If your plugin is private, [publish it as an unlisted recipe](https://help.trmnl.com/en/articles/10122094-plugin-recipes) first.

**Step 3 - Set up the Preload app**

1. Install the Preload app from the Apps page in your TRMNL dashboard, or go [here](https://trmnl.com/apps/preload).
2. Import each order. Provide the order number and checkout email from your purchase.
3. Paste a link to each plugin or recipe you want on the devices.

Each order shows how many of its devices have **shipped**, how many are **claimed** and how many are **waiting** to preload. You can import an order before it ships.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Import the order before you hand out the devices.** A device preloads once, when it is first claimed. A device claimed before its order was imported does not preload.
{% endhint %}

For more details about the Preload app, [read the docs](https://help.trmnl.com/en/articles/17574243-preload-app).

**Step 4 - Your customer claims the device**

When a customer adds the device to their TRMNL account, your plugins appear in its playlist:

* **Recipes** install ready to go. The customer fills in any fields the recipe asks for.
* **Plugins** wait in the playlist until the customer connects their own account, for example their Google Calendar.

Each customer connects their own accounts, so you never need to manage or send us their credentials.

Changes to your plugin list only impact devices that are not yet claimed. See the [Preload app guide](https://help.trmnl.com/en/articles/17574243-preload-app) for more details.

# Introduction

If you're a company with a TRMNL plugin, you can give your customers or team a device that incorporates your platform from the moment they set it up.

Example scenario:

1. Acme SaaS has a subscriber dashboard for their email newsletter tool. Acme builds a TRMNL plugin to showcase this information.
2. Acme buys 50 TRMNL devices at [discounted volume prices](https://trmnl.com/b2b#pricing).
3. Acme puts their logo on the device's boot screen with [Tailor](https://tailor.trmnl.com).
4. Acme installs the [Preload app](https://help.trmnl.com/en/articles/17574243-preload-app) to preconfigure every device with their plugin.
5. Acme gifts devices to their top customers. When a customer claims a device, Acme's plugin is already in their playlist. They connect their Acme account, and their dashboard appears.

No API keys, no coding.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Looking for the Partners API?** We retired `POST /api/partners`. Bulk orders, promo codes, Tailor and the Preload app now do the same job, without an integration on your side.
{% endhint %}
